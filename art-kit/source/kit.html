New theme art kit

Fruit Picnic is a cute merge-puzzle game like 2048: two matching tiles join into the next one. This kit covers the art for a new theme.

The story

Plan 14 tiles that grow from small and simple (2) to a grand finale (2048), plus three bonus tiles (4096–16384) for after the win.

What to deliver

  • 14 tiles, each with its background color (page 5)
  • Game background (page 6)
  • Win and lose art (page 6)
  • Files: SVG, or PNG at 1024 × 1024 px
  • Win and lose art on a transparent background

The technical bits

The only must-haves, so the art works in the game:

  • Tiles are square and fill the whole square, background included.
  • Keep the top-left corner clear for the number badge, and key details away from the rounded corners.
  • Tiles are small on phones (about 70–110 px), so each one should be easy to recognize and tell apart.
  • Win and lose art shows at about 150 px.
